Dead Air Monitoring

What counts as loss of programme audio, on every monitored source

Every other health check on this page watches whether the service is alive. This one watches whether the audio is. A source can be running, locked and buffering happily while the station it is monitoring has gone off the air — and for an EAS station that means monitoring has silently stopped.

sec
Too short and normal programme pauses alarm; 10–30 s is the broadcast convention.
dBFS
Audio quieter than this counts as silence. Catches a stopped or muted feed.
%
Spectral flatness above this reads as noise. Off-air hiss measures 40–57%; speech and music under 1%.
Required for off-air detection on a radio source. When a station leaves the air the receiver outputs full-scale hiss, which sits far above any silence level — without this, that reads as “audio present”.
