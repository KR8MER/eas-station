How It Works

  1. A qualifying alert arrives and is held instead of broadcasting immediately.
  2. It appears on the Pending Alerts queue with a live countdown.
  3. An operator can Approve it early or Cancel it.
  4. If nobody acts, it auto-releases and broadcasts when the timer expires.
