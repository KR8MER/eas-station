Every other health check on this system is inward-facing — it reports status on a page someone has to look at. This one is the opposite: it pings an external monitoring service on a fixed schedule, unconditionally, regardless of what this box thinks of its own health. If the box loses power, loses network, or the OS wedges entirely, the pings simply stop — and the external service (not this appliance) raises the alarm.

Heartbeat Configuration

Disabled by default — an unconfigured ping URL would otherwise fail on every cycle.
Any HTTP(S) endpoint that alerts you when pings stop arriving — e.g. a free check from healthchecks.io, or your own uptime monitor / webhook.
Minimum 60 seconds. Set the external monitor's expected/grace period a little longer than this.
