Basemap Provider

Controls the map background behind the polygon on exported alert share cards (PNG/WebP/MP4 export, from an alert's Export Social Image menu). Nothing else in the app -- the in-app Leaflet maps are unaffected.

OpenStreetMap tiles are darkened after the fact and can look busy/muddy at small map sizes. CARTO's Dark Matter style is authored dark and minimal from the start, so it reads far more cleanly on the share card. If CARTO is selected but no key is saved (or a tile fetch fails), rendering falls back to OpenStreetMap automatically -- an unconfigured or bad key never breaks the map.

CARTO Dark Matter Settings

Free API key required
CARTO's basemaps are free up to a generous fair-use limit (5 million tile requests/month) with no approval queue -- request one at carto.com/basemaps/apikey. Both OpenStreetMap and CARTO must be credited, which the exported card does automatically.
