Per-Service Heartbeats

A third direction, distinct from both sections above: this box pings out like the Uptime Heartbeat page, but one heartbeat per critical service (from the System Health page), each pinged only while that specific service is active. No public URL needed. A missed ping names the exact service that failed, rather than only "something is wrong" the way one combined heartbeat's alert would.
