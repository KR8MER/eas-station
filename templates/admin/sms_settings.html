SMS Help

Getting Started with Twilio

  1. Create a free account at twilio.com
  2. Buy a toll-free or long-code phone number
  3. Copy your Account SID and Auth Token
  4. Enter the phone number in E.164 format: +15555550100

Phone Number Format

All numbers must use E.164 format:
+[country code][number]
US example: +15555551234

Toll-Free Verification

Toll-free numbers must be verified in the Twilio Console before they can send at scale. Unverified numbers are blocked by most carriers.
