1. Program Description

Who sends the messages. Messages are sent by EAS Station, LLC (doing business as EAS Station™), a private technology company that builds Emergency Alert System (EAS) monitoring and decoding software. EAS Station, LLC is not a government agency, a first-responder organization, or a public-safety answering point, and does not represent itself as one. It operates this messaging program on its own behalf, for its own subscribers.

Who receives them. Only individuals who personally submitted their own mobile number on this website and then confirmed a one-time code texted to that number. There is no other way onto the list — see Section 2.

Why they receive them. Subscribers asked to be notified when this EAS Station™ instance decodes and logs an EAS alert for the area it monitors. Each message carries the event code, alert headline, affected area identifiers, and a timestamp, so a subscriber learns of an alert their monitoring station picked up without watching the dashboard. Delivery is by Twilio over a long-code or toll-free number.

A2P 10DLC campaign classification

This program is registered under a Standard A2P 10DLC use case. EAS Station, LLC does not claim the carrier Emergency special use case, which is reserved for qualifying first-responder agencies and public emergency-response organizations registering on their own behalf.

The word "emergency" describes the subject matter of the alerts this software decodes. It does not describe the sender's status, and no claim of public-safety authority is made or implied. Messages sent through this program are informational notifications to consumers who subscribed to them — they are not official government warnings and must not be relied on as a substitute for official warnings from the National Weather Service, IPAWS, or local authorities.

No marketing, promotional, affiliate, or non-subscription messages are ever sent through this program.

2. Message Flow & Call to Action

There is exactly one way a phone number joins this program: the person who owns the number enters it themselves on the public web form and then proves they control it by confirming a texted code. Every step below happens on this website, with no login, paywall, or account required at any point.

Step-by-step opt-in workflow

  1. The visitor opens {{ url_for('sms_optin_page', _external=True) }}, reachable from the footer of every page on this site and from the button above.
  2. The form asks for a mobile number in international format and, optionally, a name. Nothing is pre-filled.
  3. Directly beneath the number field is a consent checkbox, unchecked by default, whose label is the verbatim disclosure quoted below. The form cannot be submitted until the visitor actively checks it — consent is never bundled into another action, and there is no pre-selected or implied consent anywhere in the flow.
  4. Immediately under the form, the page links to the Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy, and this page.
  5. On submit, a 6-digit one-time code is texted to the number supplied. The number is not added to the recipient list at this point.
  6. The visitor enters that code back into the form. Only when the code is confirmed — proving the person holding the handset is the person who asked for the messages — is the number added to the live recipient list.
  7. The sign-up is then recorded permanently: the exact consent text displayed, the submitter's IP address, and the submission and confirmation timestamps. The operator can produce this record for any number on request.

There is no keyword, short-code, paper form, point-of-sale, voice-IVR, or over-the-phone opt-in path for this program, and no number is ever imported from a purchased, rented, partner, or affiliate list. The web form above is the program's only Call to Action.

EAS Station™ is also self-hosted software, and its admin panel does let an operator of a separate deployment enter a recipient number directly where they hold signed written consent for it. That facility is not used as an opt-in path for this messaging program, and no number has entered this program's recipient list that way. Operators of other deployments are bound by the Terms of Use and register their own campaigns separately.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency depends entirely on the volume of EAS alerts received by the system. EAS alerts are issued by the National Weather Service and other authorized government agencies. During severe weather events or other emergencies, multiple messages may be sent within a short period. During quiet periods, no messages may be sent at all.

Message frequency varies. You may receive multiple messages per day during active alert periods.

4. Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Standard SMS and data rates charged by your mobile carrier may apply to messages received from this system. EAS Station™ and its operators do not charge any fee for SMS notifications.

6. How to Opt Out (STOP)

To stop receiving SMS messages from this system at any time, reply STOP to any message. You will receive a one-time confirmation and will no longer receive SMS notifications from this number.

You may also contact the system operator directly to have your number removed from the recipient list.

Keyword Effect
STOPUnsubscribe from all messages
STOP ALLUnsubscribe and block all future messages
CANCELUnsubscribe from all messages
ENDUnsubscribe from all messages
QUITUnsubscribe from all messages
UNSUBSCRIBEUnsubscribe from all messages

These keywords are processed automatically at the carrier level by Twilio. No additional action is needed from the recipient.

9. Privacy

Phone numbers added to the EAS Station™ SMS recipient list are stored in the local EAS Station™ database on the operator's infrastructure. Numbers are shared with Twilio, Inc. solely for the purpose of delivering SMS messages. Twilio's privacy policy applies to their processing of this data.

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Delivery through Twilio is the sole exception, and Twilio acts only as a service provider processing the number to deliver the messages you asked for. Subscriber numbers are never sold, rented, or licensed to anyone. To have your number and its consent record removed, reply STOP or email support@easstation.com.

The EAS Station™ project maintainers do not have access to phone numbers stored in any operator's deployment. See the full Privacy Policy for complete data handling details.
Twilio's privacy policy: twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy.
